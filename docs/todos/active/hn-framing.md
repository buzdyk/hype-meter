# HN Framing

## Problem

The linter-not-classifier angle is the key differentiator for HN. Everyone's seen GPTZero-style statistical detectors. hype-check is different — it doesn't guess authorship, it greps for ~60 known phrasing tics and reports a density score. This framing needs to be front and center in the blog article and HN submission title.

## Approach

- Lead with "style linter" not "AI detector" in all copy
- Contrast explicitly with statistical classifiers in the opening
- Emphasize that it's diagnostic, not prescriptive — no rewrites, just a score and a hall of shame
