# Taxonomy Writeup

## Problem

The 60 markers across 6 categories are the meat of the article. People love concrete lists of "things AI does" — the fake-casual "let's be honest", the breathless "deep dive", the AI reviewer voice "not just X, but Y." Each category captures a different failure mode of LLM prose. This needs to be written up as the article's centerpiece.

## Approach

- Dedicate a section per category with 3-5 example phrases and why they're tells
- Show real matched output from hype-check runs
- Explain what each category captures as a failure mode
