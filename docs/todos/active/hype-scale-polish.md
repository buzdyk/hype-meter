# Hype Scale Polish

## Problem

The hype scale verdicts ("Corporate keynote cosplay", "The docs have achieved sentience") are quotable and shareable. They need to be polished so they land well in the article and on HN.

## Approach

- Review the 5 levels and their verdicts for tone consistency
- Make sure the scale progression feels natural and funny
- Test whether the verdicts work as standalone quotes out of context
